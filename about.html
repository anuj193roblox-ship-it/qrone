About {{NAME}}.

A QR code is only as permanent as what it points at. Some generators make dynamic codes: the printed pattern holds the vendor's short link, and the vendor forwards each scan to your page. When a trial or plan ends, the vendor can stop forwarding.

{{NAME}} makes static codes. Your address is stored in the pattern, so there is nothing to switch off. The checker reads a code on your device and tells you which kind you have.

Coming next

Editable codes on your own domain, paid once, with an export of every redirect, so your codes keep working even if this service disappears. Not available yet.

How the checker decides

It compares the code's domain with a list of known QR-vendor short links. Every entry links to the vendor's own help or pricing page. If a domain isn't on the list, the checker says so and doesn't guess.
