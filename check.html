Static code: holds your real destination. Nobody can switch it off.

Vendor-controlled: holds a middleman's short link. If the trial or plan ends, that company can turn it off.

What the check can tell you

  • Looks static

    The code points straight at a website. No QR company sits in the middle.

  • Vendor-controlled

    The code goes through a QR company's short link that it can switch off. We show that company's own page as the source.

  • Unknown short link

    A short link we haven't verified. Someone else controls it, but we can't say who.

  • Not a web link

    Text, Wi-Fi or contact details. Nothing is redirected, so nothing can expire.
