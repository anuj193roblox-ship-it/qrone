How one-time pricing actually works

{{NAME}} runs on Cloudflare's servers. The plan we use costs a fixed $5 a month, plus about $10 a year for the domain. It includes 10 million requests a month, so serving thousands of codes costs no more than serving ten.

That means a handful of sales covers a whole year of running costs. There are no investors pushing for growth and no team to pay, so nothing forces a monthly fee on you later.

If {{NAME}} ever shuts down

We commit to three things:

  1. At least 12 months' notice, on this site and by email to every customer.
  2. Every redirect stays downloadable, on every plan, now and through the whole notice period: a spreadsheet, plus ready-made files for Cloudflare, Netlify, Apache and nginx.
  3. The redirect server's code will be published openly, so you or a web developer can run it yourselves.

Being honest about independence

Codes on {{NAME}}'s own short-link address depend on {{NAME}} keeping that address running. The three promises above are what protect them.
