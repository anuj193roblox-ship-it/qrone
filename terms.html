Acceptable use

Editable codes may not send people to phishing pages, malware, scams, illegal content, or pages that pretend to be someone else. When we confirm a code breaks this rule we switch it off straight away, and scanners see "This QR code is switched off". We may close the account without a refund.

Static codes from the home page hold the address itself and never touch our servers, so we can't switch them off. The person who makes and prints a code is responsible for where it points.

14-day refund policy

Ask within 14 days of a payment for a full refund of that payment. Your account goes back to the plan you had before, and if that is Free, your codes keep working for 14 more days so nothing you printed breaks overnight. Refunds go back to your card through Dodo Payments.

Payments and upgrades

Plans are paid once; there is no subscription and nothing renews. Upgrading costs only the difference between your plan and the new one. Dodo Payments runs the checkout as the merchant of record and handles sales tax and VAT.

If {{NAME}} shuts down

At least 12 months' notice on this site and by email, every redirect downloadable throughout, and the redirect server's code published openly. The details are in our promise.

Free plan and free tools

Free editable codes are for testing and stop working 14 days after they're made. The free static maker and checker are provided as they are; always test a printed code before printing many.

Changes to these terms

When these terms change, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects what you paid for, we email customers before it takes effect.
