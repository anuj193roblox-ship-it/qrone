What we keep, and what we don't.

The free tools: nothing leaves your device

When you check a QR code, the image is read by code running on your own device. It is not uploaded, stored or sent anywhere. To see for yourself, open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab and run a check: no upload appears.

When you make a static code, the address, Wi-Fi details or text are used on your device to draw it. They are not sent to us. The free tools need no account and set no cookies.

If you sign in

Dynamic codes need an account. For that we keep:

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in links and tell your account apart.
  • Your codes: their name, where they point, and when they were made or expire.
  • Your plan, and a record of each payment: the amount, the date and the payment provider's reference. Never your card details.

One cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is needed for the account to work and is not used for tracking or ads. Sign-in links work once and expire after 15 minutes. We store only a scrambled fingerprint of each link and cookie, never the link or cookie itself.

You can change or delete any code from your dashboard at any time, or delete your whole account there. That removes your email, codes and payment records from our database at once.

Sign-in emails are sent by Resend. It sees your email address and the email itself, including the sign-in link, under its own privacy policy. The sign-in form also shows Cloudflare Turnstile, a check that you're a person and not a script. Cloudflare reads technical details from your browser for that check, under its own privacy policy.

When someone scans a code

Our redirect server looks up where the code points and sends the scanner there. We don't record scans. Cloudflare, which runs our servers and stores the data, keeps technical request logs for up to 7 days so problems can be fixed.

Payments

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, which sells on our behalf. They see your name, email and payment details under their own privacy policy. We receive only your email, what you bought and the amount.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account in the dashboard removes your email, codes, sign-in sessions and payment records from our database straight away, and your codes stop working at once. Dodo Payments, as the seller of record, keeps its own record of your payment for tax and invoicing, under its privacy policy.

No tracking

No analytics, ad pixels or third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site. If we ever add privacy-friendly visit counts, this page will say so before it starts.

Links in codes

The checker shows the address in a code as plain text. It never opens it for you.
