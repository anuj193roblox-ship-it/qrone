Nothing leaves your device.

Images

When you check a QR code, the image is read by code running on your own device. It is not uploaded, stored or sent anywhere. To see for yourself, open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab and run a check: no upload appears.

What you type

Addresses, Wi-Fi details and logos are used on your device to draw the code. They are not sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing personal. No account and no cookies. Fonts are served from this site, not a third party. If we add privacy-friendly visit counts or a waiting list, this page will say exactly what is collected before it starts.

Links in codes

The checker shows the address in a code as plain text. It never opens it for you.
