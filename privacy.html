Privacy

Images never leave your browser

When you drop in a picture of a QR code, it is read by code running on your own device. The image is not uploaded, stored or sent anywhere. You can check this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and use the checker. You will see no upload.

The generator

The text, address or Wi-Fi details you type, and any logo you choose, are used on your device to draw the code. They are not sent to us.

What we collect

Right now, nothing personal. We don't ask for an account, and we don't set cookies. If we add privacy-friendly page-view statistics or a waiting list for future products, this page will say exactly what is collected before it starts. A waiting list would collect only the email address you type into it.

Links in decoded codes

The checker shows the address found in a code as plain text. It never opens it for you. Opening a link is always your choice.
